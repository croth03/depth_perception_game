# Reward–Damage–End Gameplay Triad

**Depth Perception** fulfills the Reward–Damage–End gameplay triad through a psychological rather than traditional combat-focused structure.

## Reward

The reward is progression itself: successfully navigating the level, reaching each psychological marker, hearing the accompanying voice reflections, and moving through changing states of Reality, Hallucination, and Lost Reality gradually reveals the story and brings the player closer to the final Door.

## Damage

Damage is expressed both mechanically and thematically. Mechanically, mistakes such as failed jumps, falling, or leaving the intended traversal space can cause the player to fail and restart from an appropriate checkpoint, creating a clear consequence for unsuccessful play. Thematically, the level explores self-reflection, doubt, distorted perception, and the difficulty of moving forward while remaining trapped by past thoughts; the world becoming less stable visually represents that internal struggle.

## End

The definitive win condition occurs when the player completes the journey and walks through the Door, leading to the final message, **“Good luck on your journey.”** Failure states can be recovered from without restarting the Unity Editor.

## Tripo Assets Used in the Shipped Level

Tripo-generated assets are actively used throughout the shipped level rather than simply stored in the project:

- **Player character** — the controllable protagonist.
- **Stone Door** — the functional end-of-level objective and win trigger.
- **Psychological stone markers/totems** — narrative transition points paired with spoken reflections.
- **Ghostly Figure/Specter** — a recurring psychological presence during the level.
- **Four rock assets** — reused as environmental terrain, platform support, physical obstacles, and visual grounding.
- **Four tree assets** — used for scale, framing, environmental depth, and atmosphere.
- **Four plant assets** — placed around rocks, paths, markers, and terrain to integrate the playable space into the valley.

These assets were generated specifically for **Depth Perception** and are visibly and functionally incorporated into the playable build.
