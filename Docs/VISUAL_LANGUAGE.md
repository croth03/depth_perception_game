# Depth Perception — Visual Language

This document defines the compact palette and composition rules. Detailed character, Door, rock, foliage, animal, fog, Tripo, and state-treatment direction lives in `Docs/ART_BIBLE.md`.

## Style statement

Grounded, sculptural 3D landscapes dissolve from believable valley stone into authored spatial contradiction, staged against deep blue-black atmosphere with restrained cyan, violet, and muted pink evidence.

## Palette

- Ink — #070913 — sky, deepest shadow, Figure.
- Night — #1A2440 — fog shadow, distant terrain, structural darks.
- Stone — #46516B — grounded terrain and readable collision surfaces.
- Mist — #7A8DA8 — atmospheric separation and soft highlights.
- Signal — #82E9EE — player readability, safe emphasis, Door light.
- Drift — #AD8CFF — Hallucination evidence and depth crossing.
- Doubt — #E790BD — Lost Reality accent used sparingly.

## Light story

One cool moonlike directional key crosses the play axis. Ambient sky is Night/Mist, ground bounce is Ink, and emissive Signal appears only where composition needs a perceptual anchor.

## State progression

### Reality

Restrained contrast, believable stone and earth, grounded horizons, shallow fog, sparse anomalies. The environment should be beautiful enough to invite exploration.

### Hallucination

Recognizable landmarks persist, but silhouettes overlap, depth alignment conflicts, forms repeat, and Drift begins crossing layers. Fog deepens but traversal remains readable.

### Lost Reality

Contradiction becomes clearer, bolder, and more legible rather than noisier. Unsupported doors, repeating arches, and routes at impossible orientations establish a new grammar.

## Asset interpretation

Use one recognizable asset library across all three states where possible. Reality establishes credible objects, Hallucination rearranges or reinterprets them, and Lost Reality may transform them more aggressively. Do not default to unrelated meshes for every state.

## Camera and composition

- Perspective projection only.
- Normal framing reads as a side-scrolling platformer.
- Major transitions orbit around the player as perceptual anchor.
- The camera reveals genuine depth; the world itself is not rotated as a trick.
- The Figure is found through composition, never a UI marker.
- Stable UI and feedback never hallucinate.

## Avoid

Bright cartoon fantasy, default gray materials, generic castles, random abstract clutter, meaningless motion, constant shake, gore, and saturated neon soup.
